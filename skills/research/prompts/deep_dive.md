# Deep Dive Guide

For comprehensive research on complex topics:

## Phases
1. **Landscape mapping**: Key concepts, players, history
2. **Systematic search**: Structured queries covering all angles
3. **Source evaluation**: Methodology, sample size, conflicts
4. **Gap analysis**: What's missing from current knowledge
5. **Synthesis**: Structured report with executive summary

## Deliverable Format
```
# Topic: [Title]

## Executive Summary
3-5 bullet points

## Background
Context, definitions, history

## Current State
- Key findings by subtopic
- Data & statistics
- Expert consensus

## Debates & Uncertainties
- Major disagreements
- Methodological disputes
- Emerging evidence

## Practical Implications
- For decision-makers
- For practitioners
- For general audience

## Sources (annotated)
Tier 1: Primary/authoritative
Tier 2: Expert analysis
Tier 3: Secondary coverage

## Recommended Next Steps
```
