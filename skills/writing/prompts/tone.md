# Tone Guide

When adjusting or matching tone:

## Tone Dimensions
| Dimension | Spectrum |
|-----------|----------|
| Formality | Casual ←→ Formal |
| Warmth | Cool ←→ Warm |
| Authority | Collaborative ←→ Authoritative |
| Directness | Indirect ←→ Direct |
| Emotion | Restrained ←→ Expressive |

## Common Profiles
- **Executive**: Formal, warm, authoritative, direct, restrained
- **Technical**: Formal, cool, authoritative, direct, restrained
- **Marketing**: Casual, warm, collaborative, direct, expressive
- **Support**: Casual, warm, collaborative, indirect, expressive
- **Academic**: Formal, cool, authoritative, indirect, restrained
- **Personal**: Casual, warm, collaborative, direct, expressive

## Adjustment Techniques
- **More formal**: Full sentences, no contractions, precise vocab
- **More casual**: Contractions, shorter sentences, everyday words
- **Warmer**: "We", "you", empathy markers, appreciation
- **Cooler**: "The system", passive where appropriate, data-led
- **More direct**: Imperatives, active voice, front-load ask
- **More indirect**: Hedging, questions, context-first

## Quick Check
Read aloud. Does it sound like the intended speaker?
